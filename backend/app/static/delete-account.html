Delete your Personal Teacher Assistant account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email thomasalvaedisonsarazin@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We reply within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, study settings, lesson history, "Not clear" feedback, test attempts and scores, progress, streak, parent links and notification tokens. This cannot be undone. Lessons and questions made for a topic are shared course material without your personal data, so they stay in the app for other students.

Deletion is completed within 30 days.
