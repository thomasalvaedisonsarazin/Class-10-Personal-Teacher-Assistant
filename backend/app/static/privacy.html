Privacy Policy – Personal Teacher Assistant

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Personal Teacher Assistant ("the app", "we") helps 10th-standard students study their syllabus with lessons and tests. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google. If you choose a password, we store only a secure one-way hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Study settings: your board, medium (English or Tamil) and preferred lesson language.
  • Learning activity: lessons you watched, "Not clear" feedback, test answers and scores, topic progress and study streak.
  • Parent links: if a student shares an invite code, we store the link between the student and the parent's account.
  • Device token: if you allow notifications, a notification token for your phone.
  • Sign-in codes: when you sign in by email we store a short-lived, hashed version of the 6-digit code.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or phone number.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show your subjects, unlock topics and grade your tests.
  • To send study reminders and progress updates (you can turn notifications off in your phone settings).
  • To let a linked parent see the student's progress (parents can only view, not change anything).
  • To keep the service safe (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts).

3. Services that help us run the app

  • Google Sign-In – to sign you in with your Google account.
  • OpenAI – to write lessons and test questions. We send only the topic and syllabus details, never your name, email or answers.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech – to create the lesson voice from lesson text (no personal data).
  • Brevo – to email your sign-in codes (receives your email address).
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging – to deliver notifications (receive your device token).
  • Our hosting and storage provider – to store the app's data securely.

These providers may process data outside India. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

4. Students and parents

The app is made for students around 15 years old. A parent or guardian should help the student set up the app and agree to this policy. Parents can follow a student's progress only after the student shares an invite code, and the student can remove a parent at any time from the Profile screen.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and learning data while your account is active. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app open Profile → Delete my account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Correct your details: change your name, medium and lesson language in the Profile screen.
  • Ask a question or request a copy of your data: email us at the address below.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords and sign-in codes are stored only as hashes. Access to the admin panel is limited to our team.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
